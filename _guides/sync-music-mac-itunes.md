If your Mac is running **macOS Mojave (10.14) or earlier**, device syncing still happens inside iTunes. This guide covers that flow.

> **Running macOS Catalina or later?** iTunes has been replaced by Finder-based syncing. See [How to Sync Music from a Mac (macOS Catalina or Later)](/guides/sync-music-mac-finder).

## What you'll need

- A Mac running macOS Mojave (10.14) or earlier with iTunes installed
- Your iPhone or iPad
- A Lightning cable
- Music already in your iTunes library

## Step-by-step

### 1. Open iTunes and connect your device

Plug the device in. On the device, tap **Trust** and enter your passcode if prompted.

### 2. Click the device icon

A small device icon appears near the top-left of iTunes, below the playback controls. Click it.

### 3. Click "Music" in the sidebar

In the left sidebar under your device's name, click **Music**.

### 4. Enable syncing

Tick **Sync Music**.

Then choose:
- **Entire music library**, syncs everything in iTunes to the device.
- **Selected playlists, artists, albums, and genres**, pick only what you want.

### 5. Click Apply (or Sync)

Bottom right, click **Apply**. If it doesn't start automatically, click **Sync**.

## Enable Wi-Fi sync

In the device's **Summary** tab:

1. Scroll to **Options**.
2. Tick **Sync with this iPhone over Wi-Fi**.
3. Click **Apply**.

The device will appear in iTunes automatically when it's on the same Wi-Fi as the Mac and plugged into power.

## After syncing

Open Stezza on your device. Your synced music appears automatically, Stezza reads from your device's Music library.

If the library looks empty, see [My Music Library Looks Empty in Stezza](/guides/library-empty-troubleshooting).

## Consider upgrading

macOS Mojave is well past end-of-support at this point. If your Mac supports a newer macOS, upgrading gets you newer features and security updates. The sync flow changes to Finder-based (see the [Catalina+ guide](/guides/sync-music-mac-finder)) but is functionally similar.
