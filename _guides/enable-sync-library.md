**Sync Library** (formerly "iCloud Music Library") is the iOS setting that mirrors your music library to the cloud and makes it available across every device you sign into with the same Apple ID, including third-party players like Stezza.

If you subscribe to **Apple Music** or **iTunes Match** and your library looks empty in Stezza, this setting is almost always the cause.

## What Sync Library does

When Sync Library is on, your device's Music library includes:

- Songs you've added to your **Apple Music** library (the "+" button in the Music app)
- Tracks matched or uploaded by **iTunes Match**
- Music you've imported on a Mac or PC, if Sync Library is also on there

When Sync Library is off, only music you've **directly synced via cable** from a Mac or PC appears in the library.

Stezza reads from your iOS Music library, so anything Sync Library puts there is automatically available to play.

## Turn it on (iOS 18+)

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Scroll down and tap **Apps**.
3. Tap **Music**.
4. Under the **Library** section, switch on **Sync Library**.

![iOS Settings → Music screen showing the Sync Library toggle in the off position]({{ "/assets/guides/settings_music_sync_library.png" | relative_url }})

The toggle is a few sections down, past *Allow Music to Access*, *Notifications*, and *Mobile Data*. Look for the **Library** heading.

> If you don't see a Sync Library toggle at all, you don't have an active Apple Music or iTunes Match subscription on the Apple ID currently signed in. Sync Library only appears when one of those is active.

## Turn it on (Mac)

1. Open the **Music** app.
2. From the menu bar, choose **Music → Settings…** (or press ⌘,).
3. Click the **General** tab.
4. Tick **Sync Library**.
5. Click **OK**.

## How long does it take?

For small libraries, Sync Library populates in seconds. For larger libraries (10,000+ tracks), give it **5 to 30 minutes** for everything to appear.

If Stezza still looks empty after that, force-quit and relaunch it so it picks up the refreshed library.
